## 11. Subscription plans

ScholarNest currently offers three plans. **The plan a School is on determines what
it can access; this is enforced in the software, not merely described here.**

### Basic Plan
Core school administration: pupils, staff, classes, attendance, examinations,
results, result tokens, ID cards, facilities and the school's settings. Priced
per pupil per term.

### Standard Plan
Everything in Basic, plus the modules gated to Standard and above: CBT and CBT
practice, parent/guardian accounts, events, news, careers, testimonials,
co-curricular activities, the public school website, assignments, library,
transport, hostel, finance, teacher diary and timetable. Priced per pupil per
term.

### Exclusive Plan
Everything in Standard, plus connecting the School's own custom domain.

> **Exclusive is not currently available for purchase.** The plan is built and
> its features work, but the Platform does not presently accept new
> subscriptions to it. Custom domains are therefore not obtainable at this time.
> This will change without these Terms necessarily changing; the plan selection
> screen is authoritative on what can be bought today.

ScholarNest may change what a plan includes. Where a change removes something
material from a plan a School is already on, we will give reasonable notice.

## 12. Payments

- Fees are stated in the plan selection screens at the time of subscribing.
- **Payment is currently by bank transfer with proof of payment uploaded to the
  Platform.** Card payment is named in the interface but is **not yet
  operational**, and no card details are collected or stored by ScholarNest.
- An uploaded receipt is screened automatically for obvious problems and then
  **reviewed by a person**. Passing the automatic screen is not approval.
- A Subscription becomes active only when the ScholarNest Team approves it. Until
  then the School's administrative features remain locked.
- Fees are stated exclusive of any taxes or bank charges unless said otherwise.

Because receipts are reviewed by hand, activation is not instant. We do not
commit to a specific turnaround time in these Terms.

## 13. Pupil capacity

Basic and Standard subscriptions include a set number of **pupil places**, fixed
when the Subscription is approved.

- Capacity counts **active** pupils. Deactivating a pupil who has left releases
  their place.
- The Platform will refuse to create or reactivate a pupil beyond the School's
  capacity. This is enforced under a database lock, so it cannot be bypassed by
  submitting twice.
- Exclusive is not sold per pupil and is not capped in this way.

## 14. Additional pupil capacity

A School may buy more places at any time by submitting a top-up with proof of
payment. Approved top-ups **add to** the existing allocation; they do not reset
or replace it. Top-ups follow the same manual review as the original payment.

The Platform lets an authorised signer — a School Administrator or a member of
staff — draw a signature, which is stored as an image and printed on documents

- A signature is always recorded against **the account that drew it**, taken

- One signer has one signature; drawing a new one replaces the old.
- A School must not use a signature belonging to any other school or to any

- Drawing a signature is a deliberate act with consequences: a document bearing
  it will be treated as signed by that person. Do not draw a signature on

**Note.** Signature images are currently stored where they can be retrieved by
anyone holding the file's address. See the *Security & Data Handling Statement*.

**A School is responsible for the correctness of every result it publishes.**

## 24. Visitor submissions

- Submissions, and any files attached to them, are visible to that School's

- Do not send anything through these forms that you would not want the School

- Reports about pupils' conduct are handled by the School, not by ScholarNest. We

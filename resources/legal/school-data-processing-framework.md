## 1. Purpose of this framework

A school using AkademicNest, and AkademicNest itself, both handle personal
information about the same people: pupils, parents and staff. This framework
sets out clearly who is responsible for what, so that nothing is overlooked.

It does not move AkademicNest's own obligations onto schools. AkademicNest's
responsibilities are set out in section 5.

## 2. Roles

| Category | Controller | Processor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pupil records | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Parent and guardian records | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Staff records | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Academic records, results, attendance | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Digital signatures | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Website content and visitor submissions | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| Job applications received through the Job Portal | **The school** | AkademicNest |
| School Administrator accounts | **AkademicNest** | Not applicable |
| Subscription and payment records | **AkademicNest** | Not applicable |
| Support tickets | **AkademicNest** | Not applicable |
| Audit records, page views, security records | **AkademicNest** | Not applicable |

**Controller** means the party that decides why and how information is processed.
**Processor** means the party that processes it on the controller's instructions.

### Areas that require judgement

- **Payment receipts.** AkademicNest decides that receipts are checked
  automatically and sets the criteria. AkademicNest is therefore treated as
  **controller** for that check, which runs on AkademicNest's own servers.
- **Security records of school users' activity.** AkademicNest decides to keep
  these for its own purposes and is treated as **controller**.
- **Website content.** The school publishes it, while AkademicNest provides the
  template and hosting. The **school** is treated as controller for the content
  and any personal information in it.

## 3. What AkademicNest processes, and on what instruction

AkademicNest processes personal information on behalf of a school only to:

1. provide the platform and the school's account;
2. store, display and organise the records the school enters;
3. generate documents from them, such as report cards, ID cards and results;
4. deliver the notifications the school sets up;
5. operate the school's public website and send visitor submissions to it;
6. back up, secure and monitor the service;
7. investigate and fix faults, including at the school's request;
8. comply with a legal obligation.

**The subscription agreement is the school's instruction.** AkademicNest will not
process a school's personal information for any other purpose. In particular,
AkademicNest will **not** use it:

- to train or improve machine learning models;
- for advertising or marketing to a school's pupils, parents or staff;
- to sell, rent or license it;
- to share it with any other school;
- for AkademicNest's own analytics beyond the operational records described in
  the Privacy Policy.

If AkademicNest is legally required to process a school's data in any other way,
we will tell the school first unless the law forbids it.

## 4. School responsibilities

A school using AkademicNest is responsible for the following matters, which only
the school is in a position to handle.

### 4.1 Lawful basis and authority
Establishing its own legal basis for recording each category of information and
having the authority to enter it. This includes deciding whether it needs
parental permission for anything it records or publishes about a pupil.

### 4.2 Accuracy
All information is entered by the school, and **AkademicNest does not verify
it.** Grades are calculated from the scores and grading bands the school sets.
Incorrect inputs produce incorrect report cards, and the school is responsible
for them.

### 4.3 Informing people
Most people whose information is in AkademicNest did not enter it themselves.
**Informing pupils, parents and staff that their information is held, why it is
held, and what their rights are is the school's responsibility**, because
AkademicNest has no direct relationship with them.

### 4.4 Managing users and access
Creating accounts, assigning roles and, importantly, **removing access promptly
when someone leaves**. A former teacher with an active account can still see
pupil records, and AkademicNest cannot know that they have left.

### 4.5 Credentials
Keeping administrator credentials secure, not sharing accounts, and instructing
users to do the same. Strong, unique passwords are essential, because a password
protects access to a school's entire pupil roll.

### 4.6 Appropriate use
Using pupil information only for the school's proper purposes, not exporting it
to insecure places, and taking care of printed report cards and ID cards, which
are no longer protected by the platform once printed.

### 4.7 Requests from individuals
Handling requests from pupils, parents and staff about their own information,
as the school is the controller. AkademicNest will help where a school needs it.

### 4.8 Retention
Deciding how long to keep records, in line with the school's legal obligations.
**AkademicNest does not delete a school's records on its own initiative** and
does not know each school's retention obligations.

### 4.9 Website content
Everything published on the school's public website, including having the right
to publish any photograph of any pupil or member of staff. **A photograph
published on a public website can be seen by anyone.**

### 4.10 Visitor submissions and job applications
Handling contact enquiries, pupil conduct reports and job applications
appropriately, including anything a member of the public reports about a child.
AkademicNest does not investigate these submissions and takes no position on
them.

### 4.11 Compliance
Complying with education law, data protection law and any other law that applies
to the school. AkademicNest provides a tool; using it does not by itself make a
school compliant.

## 5. AkademicNest responsibilities

These responsibilities apply in all cases and do not depend on the school
meeting its own.

### 5.1 Processing only on instruction
As set out in section 3.

### 5.2 Security
Implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures, as described in
the *Security & Data Handling Statement*, and continuing to improve them.

### 5.3 Confidentiality
Ensuring that anyone with access to school data is bound by confidentiality.

### 5.4 Service providers
Telling schools which service providers are used (Privacy Policy, section 8) and
remaining responsible for their performance. These currently include the hosting
provider, the email provider and the font providers.

Material changes to this list will be notified in advance.

### 5.5 Assisting the school
Helping, as far as reasonably possible, with requests from individuals, impact
assessments and regulator enquiries relating to data AkademicNest processes for
the school.

### 5.6 Breach notification
Notifying the affected school **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a
breach affecting its data, with enough detail for the school to meet its own
obligations.

### 5.7 Deletion and return
When the subscription ends, deleting or returning the school's personal data as
the school directs, subject to legal retention obligations and backups.

> **Please note.** Bulk export is not yet available, so returning data currently
> means AkademicNest arranging a copy on request.

### 5.8 Records and audit
Keeping records of processing, and providing what a school reasonably needs to
verify compliance.

## 6. Individual users

Staff, pupils and parents are responsible for:

- keeping their password confidential and not sharing their account;
- signing out on shared devices, and not using "remember me" on them;
- using the account only for the purpose their school provided it for;
- not attempting to access records they are not entitled to see;
- reporting anything that looks wrong rather than exploring it;
- for staff, treating pupil information as confidential, both on screen and on
  paper.

## 7. Responsibilities at a glance

| Question | Responsible party |
| --- | --- |
| Is a pupil record accurate? | **School** |
| Should this pupil be in the system at all? | **School** |
| Who may see a pupil's results? | **School**, within the platform's options |
| Have parents been told their data is held? | **School** |
| Is the database protected against intrusion? | **AkademicNest** |
| Are passwords stored safely? | **AkademicNest** |
| Can School A see School B's pupils? | **AkademicNest**, and the platform prevents it |
| Has a former staff member's account been disabled? | **School** |
| How long are academic records kept? | The **school** decides; **AkademicNest** carries out deletion |
| Who answers a parent's request for their information? | **School** |
| Who reports a breach of the platform? | **AkademicNest** notifies the school, and the **school** notifies its people |
| Who chose the photograph on the public website? | **School** |
| Where do payment receipts go? | They stay with **AkademicNest** and are not sent to any outside service; see Privacy Policy section 8.1 |
